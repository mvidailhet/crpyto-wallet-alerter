# Rank Trade Setups With an Additive Wallet Boost

Interesting-wallet evidence will raise a trade setup's rank rather than gate whether the setup exists. Wallet evidence tied to a setup's pair adds a wallet boost per distinct, non-ignored interesting wallet; setups with no wallet evidence keep a rank of zero and remain fully visible. Ranking always records an explanation of which wallets (if any) affected a setup, and wallets or pairs tagged `ignored` are excluded from ranking influence regardless of any evidence already recorded for them.
